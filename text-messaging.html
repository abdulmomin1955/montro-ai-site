Text messaging

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Montro-AI is a product of ARAZ Venture LLC, a Texas company. This page explains the text messages the Montro-AI AI Manager can send.

Who gets texts, and why

Only store owners and managers who sign up and give us their mobile number. The AI Manager texts about their own stores: alerts, reminders, requests for approval, and setup help. We do not send marketing or promotional texts.

How you agree

You agree to receive texts when you enter your own mobile number in Montro-AI and tick the box that says you agree to receive text messages from the Montro-AI AI Manager. Agreeing is not a condition of buying anything. You can use Montro-AI without text messages by choosing email or in-app alerts.

Message details
  • Message frequency varies with what is happening in your stores.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to stop texts. Reply HELP for help.
  • Phone support: 832-526-0617.
  • Mobile information is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
