Get remote help

A real person from our team will share your screen with you and set up your accounts, step by step. It takes about 5 minutes to get started.

You stay in control

  • Nobody can see your screen until you click Accept.
  • You can stop any time: click Disconnect, or just close the program.
  • Our helper can see your screen, move the mouse and type. If we need a file from your computer (for example to set up your register or tank monitor), we ask you first. A box shows what is allowed, and a file is copied only if you say yes.
  • Nothing is recorded in website help.

1 Download the help program

Pick the kind of computer you are using now.

Windows computer Mac (Apple chip: M1, M2, M3…) Mac (Intel)

Not sure which Mac you have? Click the Apple logo, then About This Mac, and look at “Chip”. If your computer asks whether to open a program from the internet, choose Open (Mac) or Run (Windows).

2 Open it and find your ID

When the Micro Star Help window opens, it shows a number called your ID (6 to 9 digits). Keep that window open. You will type the number in the next step.
